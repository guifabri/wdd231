NP Membership Benefits

Cost: Free (registered non-profits only)

  • Listing in the chamber member directory
  • Monthly community newsletter
  • Invitation to 2 community networking events per year

Bronze Membership Benefits

Cost: $50 / year

  • All NP benefits
  • Social media welcome mention
  • 1 free business training workshop per year
  • 10% discount on chamber event tickets

Silver Membership Benefits

Cost: $120 / year

  • All Bronze benefits
  • Rotating spotlight position on the home page
  • 2 free business training workshops per year
  • 20% discount on chamber event tickets
  • Priority booth placement at the Ureña Trade Fair

Gold Membership Benefits

Cost: $250 / year

  • All Silver benefits
  • Priority homepage spotlight + premium advertising
  • Unlimited business training workshops
  • 30% discount on all chamber events
  • VIP invitations + annual cross-border trade mission (Ureña–Cúcuta)
