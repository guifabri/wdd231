Chamber of Commerce Site Plan

WDD 231 Web Frontend Development I

Site Name

Cityville Chamber of Commerce

Site Purpose

This website serves as a digital hub for local businesses in Cityville. It aims to foster economic growth, showcase local services and members through an interactive directory, promote community events, and encourage local commerce through resource sharing and networking opportunities.

Target Market & User Personas

  • Small Business Owner (Maria): Looking to expand her local network, gain exposure, and find business growth resources.
  • Corporate Leader (Diego): Seeking local partnership opportunities and community engagement to drive economic development.
  • Resident / Visitor (Francisco): Searching for trusted local service providers, event calendars, and community information.

Design Brief

Color Palette

Primary: #003366
Secondary: #0055a5
Accent: #f4a261
Background: #f9f9f9

Typography

Primary Font: Roboto / Arial (Clean, readable sans-serif for modern layout and cross-browser accessibility).

Site Map

Structure of the main pages included in the Chamber site:

  • Home
  • Discover
  • Directory (Business Directory)
  • Join

Wireframes

Desktop Layout

Grid view setup with navigation header, view toggles, and multi-column business member cards.

Mobile Layout

Single-column stacked design optimized for mobile screens with interactive toggle menu navigation.
